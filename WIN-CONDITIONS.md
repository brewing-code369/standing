## STANDING

A membership checkout for platforms that sell other people's recurring work (creator marketplaces, course and
community platforms, API marketplaces). The fan signs once; that one Tempo transaction installs three access keys on
the fan's own account: one that can only pay the creator, at most the creator's share per period; one that can only
pay the platform, at most its cut per period; one that can only pay STANDING, at most the STANDING fee per period.
Each renewal is a pull straight from the fan to each party, run by a renewal runner that holds only those
recipient-bound keys. The platform never holds the creator's money, can never take more than its cut, and cannot
point the creator's share anywhere else; the chain refuses it. The fan cancels every key with one signature.

Deviation from research: four. (1) Not atomic: each share is its own transaction, so a fan who can cover one pull and not the other pays one party (the runner pulls the creator share first).
