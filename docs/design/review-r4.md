## Questions

1. Designed product or styled document? Designed product on `/`, `/p/...` and `/new`. The line rows, receipt, stepper and live split preview come from the content. `/m/...` is a product top glued to an unbounded log, with a template plate at the end.
2. Primary job obvious in 3 s? Yes once the ledger has loaded (`home-full-1440.png`, `r4-390.png`): a large count, "0 over the cap", then a one-sentence job. No on the 1440 first-viewport capture. In that 3 seconds the reader sees three black squares, a greyed button and an unbacked green claim.
3. Would a competent practitioner produce exactly this without the content? The cheat receipt with asked-vs-cap and the overshoot bar, the refusal codes under each cap, and "LEFT THIS PERIOD 0.00 of 0.80" would not appear by default. The member page's stat-card trio, yellow gridded plate and 2x2 option grid would.

## Three highest-leverage fixes

1. Kill the hero loading state at its root. Serve the home proof from the cached `/api/stats` (commit 7727970 says it is cached server-side but undeployed: "Fly org machine limit"). Inline it at build or edge time so `.proof-num` never renders `…` at display size. Until the count exists, render "0 over the cap" from data, or not at all. It is a hardcoded literal at `Home.tsx:124` and currently shows before any log is read. Enable the cheat button from the plan's known demo member, not from ledger arrival.
2. Bound the member page and lift its actions. Show the latest 6 renewals with "all 151 in the ledger" (or paginate) so the page is about 2,500px, not 14,324px. Move "try to cheat this member" up beside the line rows, ideally as a "try it" affordance on each `SpendingLimitExceeded` / `CallNotAllowed` code, and reuse the home receipt for the result. Delete the yellow `.plate` and its gridlines (`styles.css:436-445`) and the 2x2 button grid. Replace the three bordered stat cards with one inline line ("151 renewals · 151.00 pathUSD pulled within caps · next in 21s") so 390 reaches the first key row in one screen.
3. Fix the checkout first screen and the repeated phrasing. On `/p/...`, cut the h1 to the plan name at h2 size ("Demo membership") and put "1.00 pathUSD every 5 minutes" as the price line. That lifts "Use a browser key" above y ≈ 400 at 390. Remove the second "every 5 minutes" on `/` and `/m/...`, and let "MODERATO" sit on the nav's first row at 390 (or carry it in the hero label, which already says "Live on Tempo Moderato").

## Numbers that look invented

- "0 over the cap" (home hero, green): a string literal at `Home.tsx:124`, not derived from the ledger. It is structurally true (the chain cannot settle an over-cap pull), but it renders before any data is read, so on the first viewport it is an unbacked claim styled as a measured result. Either compute it (count pulls whose amount exceeds the role cap, which should be 0) or label it as the protocol guarantee rather than a tally.
- Receipt "Checked at block 39,230,841": real and later than the hero's "read through block 39,230,828" in the same capture, which is consistent. The refused attempt itself has no hash (a simulated revert has none), and the receipt does not say "simulated, not broadcast". A judge may look for the tx and not find it.
- Verified and consistent: 453 complete renewals × 3 = 1,359 pulls. Member 151 renewals = 151.00 pathUSD at 1.00 each. Per-row amounts 0.80 + 0.19 + 0.01 = 1.00. `/new` preview 8.00 / 1.90 / 0.10 with the explanatory line "The 1% STANDING fee comes out of the platform cut". The R3 "999863.00 pathUSD" faucet balance no longer appears on the member page.
- `/new` "address pending" under ONLY TO on an empty form is an honest empty state, not an invented value.

Blind spot: I judged static captures and source only. I did not time first paint, so I cannot say how long the `…` state lasts on a cold load. The passkey checkout, faucet funding and owner cancel were not exercised.
