## Questions

1. Designed product or styled document? A designed product on all four routes now. `/m/...` reads as a member's account rather than a log with a template tail. The line card, receipt and stepper are built from the content.
2. Primary job obvious in 3 s? Yes on home at both widths (`r5-1440.png`, `r5-390.png`): the "455 complete renewals, 0 pulls over the cap" count, then "Three capped keys. One fan signature." with a one-sentence job. On `/p/...` it is slower. The first thing read is a filler title, and the actual first step ("Choose the key that signs") is second-tier at 1440 and below the fold line of attention at 390.
3. Would a competent practitioner produce exactly this without the content? No for the receipt, the refusal codes under each cap, the BOUND TO … matches the plan row and the live split on `/new`. Yes for the four-button cheat list and the uppercase-label-on-everything texture.

## Three highest-leverage fixes

1. Make the cheat live on the line, not in a list. On `/m/...` (and the home card), turn each red `SpendingLimitExceeded` / `CallNotAllowed` code into the trigger ("try it") and render the existing home receipt inline under that line. Delete the four-button list (`Member.tsx:57-60`). This removes the last stock pattern on the member page and ties every refusal claim to a one-click proof on the row that makes it. Also enable the home cheat button from the plan's known demo member instead of ledger arrival (`Home.tsx:107`, `131`).
2. Fix the checkout first screen for real. Set the `/p/...` title to "Demo membership" at h2. Put "1.00 pathUSD every 5 minutes" as the price line and replace the list-join subline with the one sentence that matters ("Three keys, one signature: 0.80 to the creator, 0.19 to the platform, 0.01 to STANDING"). Target: "Use a browser key" above y = 400 at 390. In the same pass, drop "Cancel" and "Renewal history" to h3 on `/m/...`, and close the ~150px void above Cancel.
3. Unify the bar's meaning and stop uppercasing hex. Pick one encoding for the line bar on all routes, either cap (yellow) with the pulled amount overlaid in black on `/m/...`, or the same two-tone everywhere with the legend in the card header. Exempt addresses and plan ids from `.label` text-transform (wrap them in a mono span with `text-transform: none`) so "0xC18a…F7A6" keeps its checksum casing. Fill or remove the empty left half of the "ONE FAN SIGNATURE…" band (move the plan title and price into it).

## Numbers that look invented

- "0 pulls over the cap": no longer a literal, but the check cannot fail. `Home.tsx:106` counts settled `TransferWithMemo` pulls whose amount exceeds the role share. An over-cap pull reverts on chain and never emits a log, so this set is empty by construction. It also compares single pulls, not the per-period total, which is what the cap limits. It is true, but it is a guarantee dressed as a tally. Either label it as the protocol rule ("the chain refuses any pull over the cap") or count something that can be nonzero, such as refused attempts the runner has recorded, shown next to 0 settled over cap.
- Verified and consistent: 455 × 3 = 1,365 and 458 × 3 = 1,374 capped pulls (counts rise between captures as the runner ticks). Member 153 renewals = 153.00 pathUSD at 1.00 each. Member history rows are exactly 5 minutes apart (11:18:56, 11:13:56, 11:08:56…). Per-row amounts 0.80 + 0.19 + 0.01 = 1.00. `/new` preview 8.00 / 1.90 / 0.10 = 10.00 at 20% cut, with the 1% fee taken from the platform side. The receipt's "Checked at block 39,231,758" is later than the hero's "read through block 39,231,747" in the same capture.
- Receipt "Asked 0.38 pathUSD" is exactly 2 × 0.19, matching "twice its cut". The refused attempt has no tx hash and the receipt still does not say it was simulated rather than broadcast. A judge clicking the block will not find the attempt.
- `/new` "address pending" under ONLY TO on an empty form is an honest empty state, not an invented value.

Blind spot: I judged static captures and source only. I did not time first paint, so I cannot say how long the skeleton shows on a cold load. The member-page cheat result, passkey checkout, faucet funding and owner cancel were not captured or exercised.
