## Questions

1. Designed product or styled document? A designed product on all four routes. The line card with refusal codes and inline Try it, the receipt, and the BOUND TO … matches the plan row are built from the content.
2. Primary job obvious in 3 s? Yes on home at both widths: "467 complete renewals, each pull inside its cap", then "Three capped keys. One fan signature." Slower on `/p/...`, where a filler title leads and the first action sits at y ≈ 551 at 390. On `/m/...` the 3-second read in `member-full-1440.png` is "Active" plus a red failure box, which is contradictory.
3. Would a competent practitioner produce exactly this without the content? No for the per-code Try it with its inline receipt, the overshoot bar and the key rows. Yes only for the uppercase-label-on-everything texture.

## Three highest-leverage fixes

1. Make the member page incapable of contradicting itself. When a background re-read fails and `mem` already exists, keep the data, mark it stale ("keys last read 40s ago, retrying"), and keep the full red Notice for the case where there is no data at all (`Member.tsx:95`, `:211-217`). In the same pass, give every refusal code on the card a Try it, or remove the codes that have no attack wired. Add `creator:SpendingLimitExceeded` (creator over-cap) and the two STANDING-line attacks to `TRY` (`Member.tsx:127`), or change the header to name what is pressable. Pass `block` into the member `CheatReceipt` (`Member.tsx:120`) as home does. Delete the dead `ATTACKS`/`Cheat` code (`Member.tsx:17-71`).
2. Make the overshoot receipt show the real reason. Draw the bar against what remained this period, not against the full cap. When the key has already pulled its cap, the yellow segment is 0 and all of 0.38 is hatched. Alternatively, have the home cheat ask for 2× the cap while the cap is still unspent. Then the bar and "SpendingLimitExceeded: over the cap" tell the same story. Shorten the receipt-title address (`short()`, mono). Keep the cheat button ghost after the press so "Join the demo membership" remains the only filled CTA.
3. Finish the checkout first screen and the 390 wraps. Set the `/p/...` title to "Demo membership" (one line), make "1.00 pathUSD every 5 minutes" the price line, and cut the subline to "0.80 creator, 0.19 platform, 0.01 STANDING, one signature". Target: "Use a browser key" above y = 400 at 390. Give `.btn--xs` `white-space: nowrap` so "Try it" never splits. Wrap the plan id in the home and checkout labels in a `.mono` span so it keeps its case. Close the ~180px gap above Cancel.

## Numbers that look invented

- "1,383 capped pulls settled" (and 1,401 in `r6-1440.png`) is `complete × 3` (`Home.tsx:126`), a derivation and not a count. It sits beside a ledger whose newest row is "1 OF 3 SETTLED", so the real number of settled pulls at that moment was at least 1,384. Either count the settled `TransferWithMemo` logs, or word it as "three capped pulls per renewal".
- The home receipt "Asked 0.38 / Cap 0.19" with a half-yellow bar implies 0.19 of the request was allowable. The chain's stated reason ("already pulled its cap for the period") means 0.00 was allowable. The bar's yellow segment depicts capacity that did not exist.
- Verified and consistent: 461 × 3 = 1,383 and 467 × 3 = 1,401. Member 155 renewals = 155.00 pathUSD at 1.00 each. Member history rows are exactly 5 minutes apart (11:28:56, 11:23:56, 11:18:56…). Per-row amounts are 0.80 + 0.19 + 0.01 = 1.00. LEFT THIS PERIOD 0.00 of 0.80 / 0.19 / 0.01 matches the fully black bars. `/new` preview 8.00 / 1.90 / 0.10 = 10.00 at 20% cut, with the 1% fee taken from the platform side. Receipt "Checked at block 39,232,427" is later than the hero's "read through block 39,232,415" (1440), and 39,232,452 > 39,232,441 (390).
- `member-try-1440.png` receipt copy ends "the block is when it was checked" with no block row. Source commit 753a453 makes that sentence conditional (`refusal.tsx`, `{blk ? ' The block is when it was checked.' : ''}`). Either the capture predates the deploy or the preview is behind `main`. I did not confirm which.

Blind spot: I judged static captures and source only. I did not reproduce the keychain read failure, so I do not know how often the 10s poll fails on the preview. The creator Try it, the outsider Try it, passkey checkout, faucet funding and owner cancel were not captured or exercised.
